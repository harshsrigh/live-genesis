## What is this?

Once a month, a real person sits down and types the same handful of questions into the big AI chat apps. By hand. One fresh chat per question.

Then we put the replies next to each other and keep them. Forever.

Come back next month and the same question is there again. Sometimes the answer isn't.

## The kind of questions we ask

- Is a hot dog a sandwich?
- Roast the other AI apps in one sentence.
- Pineapple on pizza: defend it or destroy it.
- One horse-sized duck or a hundred duck-sized horses?
- What's the most overrated thing humans do?
- Is Monday really necessary?

Nothing serious. Nothing political. Just the stuff you'd actually ask at 1 a.m.

## Why you'll want to come back

- **They don't agree.** Ask three AIs the same thing, get three personalities.
- **They change their minds.** We keep every month, so you can catch it happening.
- **There's a human in the mix.** One answer comes from real people. See if you can tell.
- **It takes ten seconds to read.** One short sentence each. No essays.

## How we keep it honest

- Every AI answer is typed in by hand and copied exactly. We don't fix, trim or polish a single word.
- Everything an app said is labelled with the app and the date.
- When an app refuses or dodges, we leave it out.
- AI answers can be wrong. That's half the fun, but don't take advice from them.

## Got a question for them?

[Open an issue](https://github.com/harshsrigh/live-genesis/issues/new) with your question. The good ones go on the list. Keep it fun, keep it kind, no politics.
