Our house rules

Same wordsEvery app gets the exact same words, every month. You can read them on each question's page. We ask for one short sentence, which is why the replies are short.
Not a word changedWe keep each reply exactly as it came. The page may fold a long one, but the words never change.
No answer? We leave it outWhen an app refuses or dodges, we make a note and leave it off the page.
It depends on the dayAccount, country, search on or off: any of these can nudge a reply. We write them down and tell you when something was different. So a new answer does not always mean the app changed its mind.
Humans tooThe Human answers were written by people. Quoted means one person's exact words. Summed up means we boiled several replies down to one line. No names.
Not affiliatedWe're independent, with no ties to the companies named. Their names only tell you which app answered.
These answers are AI-generated and shown exactly as they came. They can be wrong, so please don't take them as advice. We're not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Microsoft, Google, IBM or Alibaba; product names belong to their owners.When an app refuses or dodges, we leave it out.
