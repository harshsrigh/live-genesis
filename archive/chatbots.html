The rules we keep

By handA person types every question, in a new chat each time. Nothing here talks to these apps.
Same wordsExactly what we asked is on each question's page. It asks for one short sentence, so the replies are short.
Copied, not editedThe whole reply is pasted as it came. The page may shorten what it shows; the words are never changed.
Only real answers shownIf an app refuses or dodges, we keep that on file and leave it off the page.
You're trusting usThere's no receipt for a reply typed in by hand. We keep screenshots; you can't check them.
It depends on the dayAccount, country, search on or off: all of it can move a reply. We write it down, and say so when it differs.
Humans tooThe Human answers were written by people and picked by hand: one person's words (quoted) or our summary of several (summarised). No names.
Not affiliatedWe're not connected to the companies named. Their names say which app was asked, nothing more.
